<div class="section-card">
<h3>Teaching Profile</h3>

<p>
My teaching focuses on quantitative methods, causal inference, register-based research, health services research, and the application of empirical research designs to real-world health and policy questions. I emphasize transparent methodological reasoning, applied data analysis, and the translation of statistical methods into meaningful evidence for health research and health policy.
</p>
